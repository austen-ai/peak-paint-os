COULD IT RUN FOR NOTHING?

Technically yes. The free tiers cover 500 MB of data and 5 GB of traffic a month, which Peak would not get near for a long time.

The catch: a free database pauses after seven days without use. Nothing is lost and it restarts from the dashboard, but a quiet week over Christmas means the crew arrive Monday to an app that will not load.

WHY PAID IS THE RIGHT CALL

Twenty-nine US dollars a month is less than one hour of a painter’s charge-out. For that the system is always up, the code stays private, and nobody has to think about it.

Costs rise only if the business grows a long way past where it is now — and by then the system will have paid for itself many times over.

In plain terms: about NZD $600 a year to run, at cost, whoever pays it. That is the entire ongoing expense of the system.
