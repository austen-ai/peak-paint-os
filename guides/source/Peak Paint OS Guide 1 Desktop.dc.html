THE ONE-LINE VERSION

Price from the rates library, let the crew mark off what they have painted, and the report, the claim and the invoice build themselves.

ACCESS & SIGN-IND E S K T O P

Three levels. Sign in as yourself.

HQ has its own sign-in, separate from the phone app. What you see depends on who signs in — a painter cannot open HQ at all.

THE HQ SIGN-IN

Only Admin and Foreman are listed. Each needs a 4-digit PIN, set in Settings → Crew & employees.

SAME APP, TWO MENUS

The foreman menu has no Quotes, Tendering, Invoicing, Reports or Settings.

BEFORE THE CREW START

01 · SETTINGS & RATESD E S K T O P

Set these two up first.

Settings prints on every PDF the system makes. The rates library prices every quote, quick quote and variation.

SETTINGS → COMPANY DETAILS

Fill it once — it carries onto quotes, invoices, H&S packs and reports.

RATES LIBRARY

One line per thing you sell. Base rate is your cost-plus starting point; the loading factor produces the charge-out rate that quotes actually use.

Your call, Danny: are these the right rate lines and loadings for Peak, and is anything you price regularly missing from the library?

02 · QUOTESD E S K T O P

Build a quote from rate lines.

Quotes → New quote. Enter client and site, then add a line per rate code and type the quantity. Nothing is priced by hand.

THE PRICED LINES

Quantity × charge-out rate = line total. Change a quantity and the totals move with it. First four of this quote's seven lines shown.

THEN
1Write the inclusions and exclusions in plain sentences
2Download the quote PDF and send it
3Set the status to Sent, then Accepted or Declined

Your call: does the quote PDF read the way you want a Peak quote to read, and are the standard exclusions the ones you use?

03 · PROJECTS & SCHEDULED E S K T O P

Accepted quote becomes the job.

The quote total becomes the contract value. From there the project header is the one place that shows where the job stands.

Site, status, contract value and live percentage complete, with the report and claim buttons on the right.

Contract summary — value, variations, invoiced to date, balance.

Schedule — drag a bar to move the job, drag its edge to change duration. The crew initials on the bar are who sees it on their phone.

Your call: is a week-at-a-time board how you plan crew, or do you need a longer horizon?

04 · HEALTH & SAFETYD E S K T O P

Documents out, sign-ons back.

Write the task analysis for the site, generate the SWMS, and it appears on the crew's phones. Their sign-on comes straight back here.

Safety documents for the job — task analysis, SWMS, and the pack to download.

Today's sign-on log — who signed, which job, what time.

1Pick the hazards for this site from the hazard library
2Generate the SWMS before day one
3Check the sign-on log each morning
4Download the pack when a client or main contractor asks

Your call: does this cover what Site Safe and your main contractors actually ask Peak for?

05 · VARIATIONS & MATERIALSD E S K T O P

Extra work, priced and on the record.

The crew flag extra work from site with photos and a rate. It lands on your dashboard to price, approve or decline.

THE REGISTER FOR THE JOB

Area, description, value, status. Approved values are added to the contract sum; drafts are not.

WHAT YOU DO WITH IT
1Open the flagged variation from the dashboard
2Check the photos and the rate, adjust the value
3Approve or decline — approved ones show on the next report
4Materials requests arrive the same way, tied to the job

What the crew fill in on the phone — area, description, a rate from your library, quantity and photos — is shown in Guide 2, page 05.

Your call: should a variation be sendable to the client for written approval before the crew start it?

06 · TIMESHEETSD E S K T O P

Approve the week, then export.

Clock-ins arrive from the phones. Overtime past 8 h a day is calculated at 1.5×, vehicle km at $0.83.

The week in six numbers. Utilisation is hours billable to a job over hours paid — the one to watch.

Hours by crew and job, with cost and charge-out. Click a row for clock times, breaks and GPS.

Your call: Export gives a payroll CSV, a job-cost CSV, the raw grid and a signed PDF. Which format does your payroll actually need, and are the cost rates and the $0.83 km rate right?

Claim what the crew have marked off.

Send the progress report first, then raise the claim off the same percentage. The amount is worked out for you.
